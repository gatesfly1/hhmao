# How to download iFunny videos?

**iFunny** is a popular overseas community for funny content — packed with memes, GIFs and short videos. Found a video you like and want to keep it? Just two steps.

## Step 1: Copy the video link

Open iFunny and find the video you want. Below the player, click **Copy link**. The page will show "link copied" at the top when it's ready.

## Step 2: Paste and download

Open **Meowload** and pick **Link download** in the left menu. Paste the copied link into the input box and click the green **Extract** button.

The app parses the link and lists every downloadable media (video and cover image). Pick "Download video only" or "Download all" — the files go straight to your computer.
