# Bulk Video Downloader — save video and images from many platforms in one click

## Overview

The **multi-link batch download** feature lets you paste dozens of share links from different sites at once. Meowload automatically parses every link and pulls out the media inside, including:

- 🎬 Video files
- 🖼️ Image files
- 🎵 Audio files
- 📡 Live streams
- 📝 Subtitle files

## Steps

### 1. Paste multiple links

Say you found videos you like on Twitter, YouTube, and Instagram. Just paste all those share links into Meowload. The app automatically recognizes and parses each one.

### 2. Filter and download

After parsing, you can:

- **Filter by type** — show only video, image, or another type
- **Select manually** — tick the items you want
- **One-click all** — download everything in one go
