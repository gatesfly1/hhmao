# Account & Membership FAQ

Common questions about Meowload accounts and memberships — sign-in limits, plan selection, password errors, and more.

## How many devices can I sign in on with a Personal account?

A Personal account can be **signed in on at most 2 devices at the same time**. Accounts registered directly on the website are Personal accounts and are subject to this concurrent-device limit. The limit is in place to keep accounts secure and ensure fair use.

If you need more than 2 devices (for example, a team or business scenario), please contact customer support to upgrade to a **Team account**.

### A note on security

If we detect abnormal concurrent sign-ins (such as logging in and out frequently in different places), the system may restrict your account as a precaution. Please keep your credentials private.

## Do you offer a Team / Business plan?

Yes — Meowload offers a **dedicated Team plan** for teams and businesses. Please contact customer support to purchase.

## "Wrong account or password" when signing in?

**Common causes and fixes:**

| Problem | Symptom | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| **Case sensitivity** | Password has uppercase letters but you typed all lowercase | Watch caps-lock and re-enter carefully |
| **Email suffix typo** | `.com` typed as `.con` or other | Double-check the email suffix, especially `.com`, `.cn`, etc. |
| **Mixed punctuation** | Password uses English punctuation but you typed Chinese | Turn off Chinese IME, switch to English input, re-enter |
| **Fullwidth vs halfwidth** | Password uses halfwidth but you typed fullwidth | Make sure your IME is in English (halfwidth) mode |

### Tip

If your password contains special characters, verify your input mode first:

- **English IME** (recommended): punctuation renders as halfwidth
- **Chinese IME in fullwidth mode**: punctuation is widened and easy to mistype

### Still can't sign in?

After checking the above, click **"Forgot password"** to reset, or contact customer support for help.
