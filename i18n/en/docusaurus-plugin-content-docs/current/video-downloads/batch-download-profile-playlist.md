# How to batch-download videos, audio and images from a profile, playlist, channel or hashtag page?

Love a creator and want to keep their work for later — but tired of saving one by one?

The [Meowload](https://hhmao.cn/app) desktop app provides a **Profile / playlist batch download** feature. Paste a creator's profile link and the app automatically extracts every available video, audio and image, then downloads them in one click. The feature supports 1,000+ sites, including but not limited to:

- **YouTube** — channel pages, playlists, Shorts
- **Instagram** — user profiles, hashtag pages
- **TikTok** — user profiles, hashtag pages
- **Bilibili** — user profiles, collection pages
- **SoundCloud** — author profiles, playlists, public tracks in albums
- And Twitter (X), Twitch, Weibo, AcFun, and many more

## Steps

### Step 1: Copy the target page link

Open the creator's profile, playlist, channel or hashtag page in the browser and copy the URL from the address bar.

### Step 2: Paste and extract

Open the Meowload desktop app, choose **Profile batch download** in the left menu, paste the link, and click **Extract**.

The app extracts content page by page, newest first. Click **Load next page** to add pages one by one, or pick **Load 5 pages at once** / **Load all** to grab everything.

### Step 3: Sort, filter and batch download

After extraction, you can fine-tune the result:

- **Sort** — newest by default; also by views or likes
- **Filter** — by file type: video, audio, image, subtitle

Click **Download all** to save everything locally. Or use shortcuts like **Download video only** to save just one type.
