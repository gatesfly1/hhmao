# How to download product videos from e-commerce sites

Found a nice product video on JD, Taobao, Pinduoduo or Amazon and want to save it as material — but right-click doesn't have "Save as"? Phone screen recordings come out blurry, noisy and unusable.

The Meowload browser extension grabs the original high-definition video directly — no more blurry screen recordings.

This is a **general method**. It works not only for e-commerce product videos but for **any page that contains video, audio or live streams**.

## Install the Meowload browser extension

Install from the browser store:

- **Chrome**: [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/meowload-videoliveaudioim/gnplpcfnikehohdgnfnfcienljcbaogj)
- **Edge / Quark**: [Edge Add-ons](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/meowload-videoliveaud/mfmeocjfdcmipdcebfdgpncpmcddmech)

See [Install the Meowload browser extension](install-meowload-extension.md) for full steps.

## Download a product video

JD is used as an example below; the steps are identical on any e-commerce site.

**Step 1**: Open the product page in the browser. The extension automatically detects the videos on the page.

**Step 2**: Click the Meowload icon in the top-right corner. The popup shows the detected videos.

**Step 3**: Tick the videos you want and click **Batch download**.

**Step 4**: After downloading, click the folder icon to see your saved videos.

## Supported platforms

Works on all major e-commerce platforms, including but not limited to:

- **Domestic**: JD, Taobao, Tmall, Pinduoduo, 1688, Douyin Mall
- **Overseas**: Amazon, AliExpress, eBay, Shopee

It also works on any page with video — news sites, social media, online courses, and so on.
