# How to record Twitch live streams?

**Twitch** is the world's largest game-streaming platform, packed with amazing creators. If you want to record a stream and rewatch it later, **Meowload** makes it easy. Just two steps to save a live broadcast to your computer in real time.

## Step 1: Copy the stream link

Open Twitch and head into the streamer's channel. Copy the full stream URL from the browser address bar (usually `https://www.twitch.tv/<username>`).

## Step 2: Paste and start recording

Open the **Meowload** app, pick **Link download** in the left menu, paste the Twitch URL, and click the green **Extract** button.

The app parses the live stream and lists every available media (video stream, audio and cover image) in the panel below. The first row shows the **Live** status tag — recording is in progress. You can watch the live download speed and the recorded size grow.

:::tip
Recording continues until the streamer ends the broadcast or you stop it manually. The video is saved automatically to the download folder configured in the app.
:::
