# How to download a YouTube playlist

YouTube has millions of playlists — music albums, tutorial series, curated compilations. Saving a whole playlist locally is great for offline viewing, study, or personal collections.

With [**Meowload**](https://hhmao.cn/app) you can download a complete YouTube playlist in batch, in many formats and resolutions (including HD). Here's how:

1. **Install the desktop client**

   Download and install [**Meowload**](https://hhmao.cn/app). Available for Windows and macOS.

2. **Copy the playlist link**

   Open the YouTube playlist in the browser and copy its URL from the address bar.

3. **Paste and download**

   Paste the link to preview the available media.

4. **Load more and filter**

   Click **Load more** to pull more videos. Sort by views or likes, or use the filter to pick what you need.

5. **Pick what to download**

   Select all, or tick specific items, then click **Download selected**.

6. **Done!**

   Once downloads finish, click the folder icon to see the saved files.
