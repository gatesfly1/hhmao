# How to download videos in WeChat articles

WeChat public-account articles often embed video, but there's no built-in download. The Meowload browser extension detects videos in articles and saves them locally in one click.

## Install the Meowload browser extension

If you haven't installed it yet, head to the browser store:

- **Chrome**: [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/meowload-videoliveaudioim/gnplpcfnikehohdgnfnfcienljcbaogj)
- **Edge / Quark**: [Edge Add-ons](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/meowload-videoliveaud/mfmeocjfdcmipdcebfdgpncpmcddmech)

See [Install the Meowload browser extension](install-meowload-extension.md) for full steps.

## Download a video from a WeChat article

1. Open the WeChat article link in your browser, e.g. `https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/v0OiILpavyPe4PDHaqf_KQ`
2. Once the page loads, Meowload auto-detects the videos in the article. When the extension icon lights up, downloadable content has been found.
3. Click the Meowload icon. The popup lists the detected videos — pick one and download. When it's done, click the folder icon to view it.
